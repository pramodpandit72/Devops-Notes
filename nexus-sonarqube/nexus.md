# Nexus

## Overview
Nexus stores build artifacts like JARs and Docker images.

## Key ideas
- Acts as a private registry
- Helps control versions

## Interview questions with answers
- Q: Why use a repository manager?
	A: To store, cache, and control artifact versions.
- Q: What types of artifacts can Nexus store?
	A: JARs, npm packages, Docker images, and more.
